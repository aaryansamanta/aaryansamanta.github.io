Analysis workflow

  1. Load patient-profile CSV
  2. Inspect rows and missing values
  3. Remove duplicate records
  4. Convert age; encode blood pressure and cholesterol categories
  5. Compute summary statistics
  6. Visualize distributions, groups, diseases, and correlations

About this project

Author: Aaryan Samanta · Organization: Legend College Preparatory · Year: 2025. Original source notes that the work was adapted from classroom exercises.

The research report discusses distributions, disease frequency, and the relationship between blood pressure and cholesterol. Its reported correlation of 0.31 is a historical result from the original dataset, not a result from the synthetic example. This website does not predict diagnoses, train machine-learning models, or offer medical advice.
